# H2 sourcing list — pre-1980

- **Date:** 2026-08-02 · **Target:** H2 (subcat 49) to depth 100, currently 75
- **Airs:** Golden Hour 2/hr and Weekend Wide 1/hr — 30 slots/wk, ~560h turnover
- **Effect:** a given track surfaces roughly every 23 days. Surprise, not staple.

## Selection criteria

1. **Recognisable.** The surprise only lands if the listener knows the record. An
   obscure 70s deep cut just sounds like filler in a New Rock hour.
2. **Riff-forward and energetic.** No ballads. At 2/hr in a gold-exclusive hour this is
   seasoning — it should hit, not drift.
3. **No artist already in H2.** The existing 75 cover AC/DC, Aerosmith, Springsteen,
   Queen, Pink Floyd, Rush, Fleetwood Mac, ZZ Top, Van Halen, Heart and the rest of the
   80s AOR spine. All picks below are artists not yet represented.
4. **1967–1979, spread.** H2 today is almost entirely 1980s, which is not what the
   pre-1980 surprise was for.

## Deliberately not included

**Lynyrd Skynyrd, Ted Nugent, Peter Frampton, Grand Funk, Bachman-Turner Overdrive** —
recognisable but they pull toward Classic Hits rather than sitting *against* the format.
The surprise works when the record is great and clearly foreign; it fails when it sounds
like the station drifted.

**Slade — Cum On Feel The Noize.** H2 already carries the Quiet Riot cover. Same song.

## The higher-value list: pre-1980 for H1

The most format-coherent pre-1980 material is **alt canon and belongs in H1**, not H2.
H1 sits at 24 tracks against a 24 target, so it has no headroom — but this material is
closer to the station's actual lineage than anything in H2, and deepening H1 may be
worth more than finishing H2.

### One gap worth naming

The pre-1980 additions are **almost entirely male-fronted**. H2's only female presence
comes from the 80s side — Heart, Stevie Nicks, Fleetwood Mac, Cher. For a station whose
current roster includes Spiritbox, Halestorm, Poppy, The Beaches, The Last Dinner Party
and Hayley Williams, an all-male vintage tier is a noticeable inconsistency in what the
station sounds like it values.

Suzi Quatro is the strongest available fix — she is also the direct forebear of Joan
Jett, who is already in H1. Others worth looking for: **Fanny** (*Charity Ball*, 1971),
**The Runaways** (*Cherry Bomb*, 1976), **Janis Joplin** (*Piece Of My Heart*, 1968),
**Jefferson Airplane** (*Somebody To Love*, 1967).
